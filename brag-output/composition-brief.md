## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: Warm luxury espresso & burnt caramel mobile product film ("KŌVÉRA after sunset")
- Angle: Showcase the tactile warmth of KŌVÉRA's editorial design system alongside real interactive app flows—from browsing morning specialty edits to customizing beverage/bakery orders and confirming checkout.
- Hook: KŌVÉRA's dark espresso splash opening directly into the warm porcelain "What will you sip today?" storefront.
- Outro / punchline: `K Ō V É R A — Your Sanctuary for Premium Coffee Moments.`
- Avoid:

  - Abstract filler visuals
  - Exposing personal user email addresses
